# Sample answer — web-research

> **Prompt:** Is intermittent fasting better than calorie restriction for weight loss?

**Answer.** When total calories are matched, intermittent fasting (IF) and continuous calorie restriction produce similar weight loss in most randomised trials. IF is not clearly better; some people find it easier to stick to, others don't.

**Key findings**

- Meta-analyses of RCTs report no meaningful difference in weight loss between IF and daily restriction over 3–12 months [1][2].
- A 12-month trial comparing time-restricted eating plus restriction vs restriction alone found no significant difference [3].
- Adherence varies by person; dropout rates are similar across groups in most trials [1].

**Disagreements / uncertainty**

- Some smaller studies report metabolic benefits (e.g. insulin sensitivity) independent of weight loss; larger trials have not consistently reproduced them.
- Most trials are short and in adults without diabetes.
